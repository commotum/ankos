## Boundary Decisions

The review also forced one uniform transform/wrapper rule:

- A one-shot or derived construction is executable when the source supplies a
  closed map or relation with its own invariant operational commit.
- A role remains close when all state-changing work is delegated to supplied
  components and the wrapper contributes only interpretation or ordinary
  composition.

This keeps F004 and F045 executable, because they respectively build a
dependency graph and construct/aggregate a calibrated surrogate ensemble. It
keeps F010 close, because removing its encoder/decoder interpretation leaves
the target carrier and transitions unchanged. F042 remains close because the
cited source supplies a sampled configuration and spanning property, not an
independent construction procedure.

Other cautions were resolved as follows:

- F050 rejection now returns an explicit identity/quiescent continuation that
  advances proposal control; it is not conflated with terminal zero
  successors.
- F003 remains separate from F053 because each asynchronous write is
  immediately readable by the next selected locus. F001 remains separate
  because a whole disjoint block or checkerboard phase is one coupled commit.
- F056 starts from constructive finite approximations. It does not claim that
  an arbitrary noncomputable oracle can be serialized in `Seed` or `Rule`.
