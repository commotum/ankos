# 12-API-PRESSURE

## Current Facts

- Stage 11 retained 52 executable semantic families and three close-exclusion
  mechanics groups.
- `simple_programs.md`, `api.md`, the completed Goal 1 design conclusions, and
  the frozen Goal 2 handoff were read for this stage.
- Every family was mapped independently to `Seed`, `Alphabet`, `Frontier`,
  `Neighborhood`, and `Rule`.

## Result

- All 52 executable families fit the five-component API.
- The three remaining groups are observers or interfaces rather than new
  executable mechanics.
- No concrete construction requires a sixth top-level field.
- No construction requires a public `UpdatePolicy`.
- The current `simple_programs.md` representation is too narrow even though its
  writable-frontier and atomic preserve-outside semantics are sound.
- The frozen Goal 2 handoff must be remastered before implementation because it
  adds a public `UPDATE` axis and segregates constraints, functions, and PDE
  relations into a sibling ontology.

The complete family-by-family mapping, contract revisions, rejected
extensions, and Goal 2 consequences are in `api-pressure.md`.

## Decisive Contract Changes

- `Seed` becomes a source of typed exact, constructive, partial, or
  probability-law configurations. Generated configurations carry support,
  topology, geometry, defaults, and invariants.
- `Alphabet` becomes a closed structural value schema rather than only a finite
  palette.
- `Frontier` remains the complete possible-write envelope and may be dynamic,
  structured, intensional, continuous, or include fresh components.
- `Neighborhood` remains the read capability and may be nonlocal, global,
  historical, structural, or differential.
- `Rule` becomes a closed relation returning zero, one, many, distributed, or
  symbolic complete frontier replacements with typed outcomes and witnesses.
- Atomic application and preservation outside the frontier are universal engine
  semantics, not configurable program policy.

## No-Cheating Checks

- API fit was tested after discovery and family identity were frozen.
- Semantic names were not treated as evidence for public classes.
- Relations and one-shot evaluations were tested as mechanics rather than
  rejected for lacking a conventional discrete loop.
- Every putative extra axis was rejected only after locating its semantics in a
  program component, the universal engine, the run request, or tooling.
- No catalog, API, runtime, test, Book, or predecessor-goal file was modified.
