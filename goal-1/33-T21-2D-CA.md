## Updated Assumptions

- Four-neighbor geometry means the orthogonal displacement set `{(-1,0),(0,-1),(0,1),(1,0)}` in a declared coordinate orientation. Center inclusion is a RULE/read-schema choice, not an unspoken fifth direction.
- A totalistic summary is a lossless closed RULE representation only when the exact valuation, included positions, aggregate, reachable aggregate values, row order, and output table are explicit. It must not become a family executor.
- The main text uses “totalistic” loosely for the ten cases obtained from `(center value, number of black cardinal neighbors)` (`BOOK:2170,2194`). The Notes distinguish this as outer totalistic and reserve totalistic for the six cases obtained by summing all five values (`BOOK:13534-13549`). Goal 2 must preserve the two case schemas rather than normalize the terminology by erasing their difference.
- T22's diagonal-inclusive Moore neighborhood, T23/T24 dimensions, other lattices, Life, and weighted/outer-totalistic variants are boundaries or parameterizations to audit, not evidence that strict T21 silently includes all of them.
- Infinite blank support, finite windows with exterior reads, periodic tori, and cropped renderings are not interchangeable. Native support and computation realization must be stated separately.
- A growing black region is a changing subset of labels on one fixed grid, not growth of configuration support. Sparse quiescent storage is an optional lossless realization only for a proved background-preserving program.
- Synchronous assignment is defining semantics. Orientation, display, growth shapes, slices, density plots, and behavior classes remain observations unless they feed the rule.
