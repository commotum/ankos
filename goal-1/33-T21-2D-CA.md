## Construction Model

- DOMAIN: discrete `t+2D`; no square-grid support or array rank is folded into this dimensional declaration.
- Configuration/support: a total labeled fixed square-grid `Z^2` support/topology. Any finite computation realization, periodic quotient, exterior policy, uniform-background representation, axis map, or viewport is explicit.
- ALPHABET: finite typed labels; the strict direct profiles are binary, while T02 supplies the independently evidenced finite-alphabet parameterization.
- FRONTIER: all sites in one old time slice.
- NEIGHBORHOOD: an explicit composition with exactly one `SelfAt` access and four distinct ordered cardinal-offset accesses. In the Book codec frame the five declared positions are `Offset(-1,0), Offset(0,-1), SelfAt, Offset(0,1), Offset(1,0)`. The center is visible because the access schema declares it, never because RULE reaches around NEIGHBORHOOD. Diagonal access is a separate T22 parameter.
- RULE: one of three distinct closed descriptions over that five-position read: (a) a complete 32-context ordered table; (b) the chapter's 10-case product of the declared self value and exact four-cardinal count; or (c) the later six-case equal sum of the declared self plus four cardinal values. Compact forms are validated factorized representations of qualifying exhaustive tables, never aliases for each other.
- UPDATE: one same-site label write per firing locus, committed snapshot-parallel while support/topology remain fixed.
- Successor: one deterministic configuration per valid event; fixed points do not imply native halt.
- Seed/realization: separate exact initial configuration, constructors/laws, finite work window, boundary/exterior values, horizon, and viewport.
- Observers/relations: slices, outlines, areas, growth rates, behavior classes, invariant-state constraints, art, biology, emulations, and renderings do not feed execution.
