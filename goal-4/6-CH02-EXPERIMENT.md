  general rule schemas, named presets, seed/boundary classes, result queries,
  representations, properties, and historical applications distinct.

  immutable query, or observer. It need not be forced into an iterated
  trajectory to receive blind coverage.

  every implementation detail. Missing mechanics remain explicit rather than
  being reconstructed from familiarity or pixels.

  a substitute for it.
