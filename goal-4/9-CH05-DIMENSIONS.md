## Updated Assumptions And Findings

- Dimension is not a sufficient semantic identity. The audit keeps a native
  carrier and update separate from its slice, projection, stacking, path,
  quotient, layout, scan, history embedding, or dimensionality observer.
- The assigned source independently supports construction-bearing records
  across spatial cellular automata, two-dimensional Turing and mobile
  automata, substitutions and geometric replacement, graph and network
  systems, multiway rewriting, grammars and games, local constraints,
  PDE/vector/variational relations, tilings, sequence restrictions,
  Diophantine relations, and formula search.
- Uniterated generators, codecs, denotations, predicates, direct functions,
  solvers, constraints, observers, representations, and native iterated
  systems remain distinct. In particular, a declarative solution set or
  direct evaluator was not forced into a trajectory model.
- A rule-number codec is not the native cellular-automaton update it
  describes; a constraint predicate is not its backtracking solver; a
  network layout is not its graph evolution; a multiway state-count function
  is not the branching rewrite relation; and a finite pattern generator is
  not silently identified with a substitution trajectory.
- Search recovered several omissions that sequential disposition alone did
  not delimit cleanly, including rule codecs and family-cardinality queries,
  trajectory and dimensionality observers, cut-and-project generators,
  direct graph queries and transforms, exact multiway count functions,
  grammar presets, direct/solver distinctions for vector equations,
  constraint decoders and predicates, finite-element discretization,
  Diophantine generators/solvers, scan orders, Sierpiński generators,
  history embeddings, and pattern-count observers.
- Candidate count is deliberately not a semantic-family count. No blind
  candidate was merged, mapped to a T identifier, assigned an API fit, or
  given a final catalog/family action in this stage.
